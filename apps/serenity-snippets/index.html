    How to use

    • Click any snippet to copy it, then paste it into a note with Ctrl+V.
    • Search: start typing to see suggestions. Use the arrow keys and press Enter to copy.
    • Favorites: click the star on a snippet to pin it to the top.
    • Recently used snippets show up at the top automatically.
    • My Snippets: click + New snippet to write your own, or the pencil on any snippet to make your own version.
    • Fill-ins: text in [brackets] is a reminder to fill in details after you paste.
    • Keyboard shortcuts: click the keyboard icon on a snippet and press a combination like Alt+Shift+1. Then, in any text box on any website (Axiscare, email, etc.), press it to type the snippet right where your cursor is.
    • Open this panel: press Alt+Shift+S from any page.
    • Keyboard shortcuts: click the keyboard icon on a snippet and press a combination like Alt+Shift+1. While this page is open and in front, the shortcut copies the snippet. Install the browser extension to use shortcuts directly inside Axiscare and email.

    Privacy: everything is stored only on this computer, in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. Use Back up my snippets to move your snippets to another computer.
